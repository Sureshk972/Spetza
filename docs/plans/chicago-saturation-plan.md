# Spetza — Chicago saturation plan

*Zip-code-level expansion playbook. Solo operator. Density before breadth.*

## The core problem

Spetza is a two-sided marketplace. It's worthless to senders if no courier is nearby, and worthless to couriers if there are no deliveries to grab. You can't launch "Chicago" — you launch a zip code.

## The rule

**Never expand to a new zip until the current zone can reliably accept a delivery within 30 minutes.** If a sender posts and nobody picks up for 2 hours, they never come back. One bad experience kills word-of-mouth in a small area.

---

## Phase 0 — Seed (1 zip code)

**Zone: 60640** (Andersonville / Uptown)
- Why: Your couriers are already here. You know the neighborhood.
- Population: ~60,000. Dense, walkable, mix of residential + small retail.

### Supply side (couriers) — build first
| Target | How |
|---|---|
| 5 active couriers | Personal outreach: gig workers, college students, retirees |
| Recruit where they already are | Flyers at coffee shops on Clark St, Andersonville bars, Uptown transit hub |
| Pitch | "Earn $8.50–$30 per delivery on your own schedule. Already driving? Pick up a package on the way." |
| Activation gate | Courier must complete 1 delivery in first week or you lose them |

### Demand side (senders) — start after 3+ couriers active
| Target | How |
|---|---|
| 10 deliveries/week → 3-5/day | Start with local businesses, not consumers |
| Small retail | Andersonville shops that do local delivery (bakeries, boutiques, florists) — replace their ad-hoc delivery |
| Nextdoor / Facebook | Post in Andersonville/Uptown groups: "Need something delivered across the neighborhood? $10." |
| Residential | Flyers in apartment lobbies on Kenmore, Winthrop, Broadway |

### Metrics to track
| Metric | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Time to accept | < 30 min | Sender experience — this is the #1 metric |
| Deliveries/day | 3–5 | Enough for couriers to earn, not so many they can't cover |
| Courier utilization | 1–2 deliveries/day/courier | Below this, couriers churn |
| Repeat sender rate | > 30% | If senders don't come back, fix the product before expanding |

### Duration: 4–6 weeks
### Expansion trigger: Time-to-accept < 30 min for 2 consecutive weeks AND repeat sender rate > 30%

### How it works
- Existing 60640 couriers can service adjacent zips (2-mile delivery crosses zip boundaries)
- Recruit 2–3 new couriers per zip (total: 10–15 active)
- Demand: expand the same playbook — local businesses + Nextdoor + flyers
- Lakeview is the growth engine: highest density, most young professionals, most comfortable with app-based services

### Metrics
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|

| Time to accept | < 30 min across all 4 zips |
| Active couriers | 10–15 |
| Coverage gaps | No zip with 0 couriers online for > 2 hours during 9am–8pm |

### Duration: 2–3 months
### Expansion trigger: 15+ deliveries/day sustained, < 30 min accept in all 4 zips

## Phase 4 — Second zone or second city

Only after North Side is self-sustaining. Two options:

**Option A: South Side / West Loop**
- Jump to a new Chicago zone (60607, 60608, 60616, 60653)
- Advantage: same city, same marketing, same ops
- Risk: different demographics, different needs

**Option B: New city**
- Milwaukee, Madison, or Indianapolis (smaller, easier to saturate)
- Advantage: proves the model is repeatable
- Risk: you can't be in two cities as a solo operator

**Recommendation: Option A.** Stay in Chicago until you hit 500+ deliveries/day city-wide. You know the city, you can physically be present, and you can't manage two markets solo.

## Chicken-and-egg tactics

### The cold start problem at each phase

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Supply only | Recruit couriers. Do NOT market to senders yet. |
| Week 2 | Seed demand | You personally post 3–5 deliveries (have friends/family send packages). Couriers get their first completions and earn-back credits. |
| Week 3 | Real demand | Start flyering, Nextdoor posts, business outreach. You have couriers ready to accept. |
| Week 4+ | Flywheel | Senders post → couriers earn → couriers tell friends → more couriers → faster accept times → senders tell friends |

### Guarantees that de-risk the sender
- "Delivered in 2 hours or your fee is refunded" (you eat the $1.50 sender fee)
- Show "3 couriers nearby" on the request screen (social proof of supply)
- Push notification: "Your delivery was accepted in 4 minutes" (speed is the product)

### Community college recruitment

Students are ideal couriers: flexible schedules between classes, need side income, and concentrated in specific zip codes. Target the City Colleges of Chicago system + nearby public universities.

| Phase | School | Address | Zip | Enrollment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Seed | Harry S Truman College | 1145 W Wilson Ave | 60640 | ~4,000 |
| 1 — Ring 1 | Northeastern Illinois University | 5500 N St. Louis Ave | 60625 | ~7,000 |
| 1 — Ring 1 | Loyola University (Lake Shore) | 1032 W Sheridan Rd | 60660 | ~12,000 |
| 2 — Ring 2 | DePaul University (Lincoln Park) | 2320 N Kenmore Ave | 60614 | ~21,000 |
| 3 — North Side | Wilbur Wright College | 4300 N Narragansett Ave | 60634 | ~8,000 |

**Playbook per school:**

1. **Bulletin boards** — flyer in student centers, libraries, dining halls. Lead with earnings: "Make $50–$80/day between classes."
2. **Student job boards** — most CCC campuses and universities have an online job board or career services portal. Post as a flexible gig, not a job.
3. **Student orgs** — reach out to business clubs, commuter student groups, and international student associations (students on certain visas can do gig work).
4. **Campus ambassadors** — your first courier at each school becomes the recruiter. Offer a referral bonus: $10 credit per activated courier they refer.
5. **Semester timing** — recruit in the first 2 weeks of fall/spring semester when students are looking for income. Avoid finals and breaks.

**Why community colleges specifically:**
- Students commute (they have cars/bikes, unlike dorm residents)
- More likely to need flexible income than 4-year university students
- Class schedules have gaps — perfect for 1-2 hour delivery runs
- Truman College is literally in your seed zone

### Guarantees that retain the courier
- Earn-back: $1/delivery toward the $40 background check (already built)
- Show weekly earnings prominently (already in CourierHome)
- "Hot area" alerts when deliveries are nearby (already built)
- Referral bonus: courier refers another courier, both get a bonus delivery credit

## What you track weekly

| Metric | Healthy | Warning | Action if warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time to accept | < 30 min | > 45 min | Recruit more couriers in that zip |
| Deliveries/day | Growing week over week | Flat or declining | More demand-side marketing |
| Repeat sender rate | > 30% | < 20% | Talk to churned senders — product problem? |
| Courier online hours | 4+ hrs/day average | < 2 hrs/day | Couriers aren't earning enough — boost demand |
| Coverage gaps | < 1 hr/day with 0 couriers | > 3 hrs with 0 couriers | Stagger courier schedules, recruit for gaps |
